Privacy Policy

Pip Explains Uploader, operated by Pip Explains AI. Last updated: October 1, 2026.

What this app is

Pip Explains Uploader is a private tool run by the owner of the YouTube channel Pip Explains AI. It publishes that channel's own videos and reads that channel's own statistics. It is not a public service, and only the channel owner signs in to it.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app can access:

  • Basic channel information (channel ID, title) to confirm the right channel is connected.
  • Video upload and management: uploading videos, setting titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, playlists and publish times, and posting a comment on the channel's own videos.
  • YouTube Analytics reports for the channel's own videos (for example views, watch time and audience retention) and, if available, estimated revenue reports.

The app does not access other users' private data, contacts, email, or any non-YouTube Google data.

How the information is used

The data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to measure which videos perform well so that future videos can be improved. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and security

The OAuth access token and analytics summaries are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in files readable only by that user. Nothing is stored on a third-party server operated by this app.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any user data with third parties. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube through the official APIs in order to perform the actions above.

Cookies and tracking

The app and this website do not use cookies, analytics trackers or advertising identifiers.

Retention and deletion

Stored analytics summaries are kept for up to 12 months and are then deleted. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. After access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted. To request deletion of any stored data, email the address below; requests are completed within 30 days.

Children

This app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
